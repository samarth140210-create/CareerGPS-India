About CareerGPS India

CareerGPS India is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help Indian students make informed career decisions through personalized recommendations, roadmaps, scholarships, competitions, and intelligent mentoring.

🎯 Our Mission

We aim to bridge the gap between students and career opportunities by providing personalized guidance based on interests, skills, academic performance, and career goals.

✨ Key Features

  • AI Career Assessment
  • Personalized Career Recommendations
  • Step-by-Step Career Roadmaps
  • Weekly Progress Tracking
  • AI Career Mentor
  • Scholarship Discovery
  • Competition Recommendations
  • Skill Gap Analysis

🚀 Why CareerGPS ?

Instead of providing generic career advice, CareerGPS creates a personalized journey for every student. The platform tracks progress, recommends the next learning milestone, and provides AI-powered guidance based on each student's profile and career aspirations.
